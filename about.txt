📌 Overview
What can 150,000+ e-commerce transactions reveal about customers, products, revenue, profitability, discounts, marketing, delivery, returns, and customer loyalty?

This E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dataset contains 150K+ transactions from 2021–2025 and provides a practical foundation for exploring the complete e-commerce business lifecycle.

Instead of looking at sales as isolated numbers, this dataset allows you to connect multiple business dimensions:

Customers → Products → Orders → Sales → Revenue → Discounts → Profitability → Marketing → Delivery → Returns → Ratings → Loyalty

Whether you are learning Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, statistics, machine learning, or business intelligence, this dataset can be used to build real-world analytical projects, dashboards, predictive models, portfolio projects, and data science case studies.

🔥 Why Explore This Dataset?
The most valuable insights in e-commerce often appear between different business variables, rather than inside a single column.

For example
📉 High-revenue products may not always be the most profitable.
🏷️ Larger discounts may increase sales while reducing margins.
📣 Some acquisition channels may bring customers with higher business value.
🚚 Delivery performance may be associated with customer experience and returns.
💎 Loyal customers may demonstrate different purchasing patterns.
📦 Certain categories may combine strong demand with higher return activity.
🌎 Regional markets may show different customer, product, and purchasing behavior.
This makes the dataset suitable for moving beyond basic descriptive statistics toward:

Descriptive Analytics → Diagnostic Analytics → Predictive Analytics → Business Intelligence

The deeper opportunity is understanding why those orders happened and what they mean for the business.

Instead of asking only
How much did we sell?

Which acquisition channels attract valuable customers?
What factors may contribute to returns?
Does delivery influence customer experience?
How does loyalty relate to purchasing behavior?

These questions turn a transaction dataset into a business intelligence and decision-support environment.
